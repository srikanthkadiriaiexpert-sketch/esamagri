What this prototype shows
  • Real design system — same colors, type, spacing and components as the production React app.
  • Strict RIK / PIF separation — try adding items in RIK, then switch to PIF: your PIF cart starts empty, and vice versa.
  • No entitlement bar — allowed/issued/reserved/remaining are shown as plain figures, per the approved direction.
  • RIK has no price or payment UI anywhere; PIF is COD-only, no fake payment processing.
  • All catalog, order and profile data here is placeholder — the real app talks to the live NestJS/PostgreSQL backend and shows honest empty states until each module is built.
