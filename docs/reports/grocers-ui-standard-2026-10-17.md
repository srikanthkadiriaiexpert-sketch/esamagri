# e-Samagri — the standing shop-screen standard

**17 October 2026.** Records the standard as it now exists in code, so the
remaining eighteen Grocers categories can be built against something
checkable rather than against a memory of a screenshot.

## The standard is one file

`apps/customer-web/src/routes/pif/business/ShopScreen.tsx`.

Grocers and Fresh Mart are the same component. Neither owns a copy of the
header, the drawer, the breadcrumb, the hero or the bottom bar, so the two
cannot drift apart as categories are added. What a business supplies is
only:

| Supplied per business | Grocers | Fresh Mart |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo | `/brands/grocers.jpg` | `/brands/fresh-mart.jpg` |
| Accent | orange | green |
| Drawer contents | the 19 main categories | the whole tree, 10 entries |
| Search wording | "Search groceries, staples…" | "Search fruits, vegetables…" |
| Below the hero | subcategory grid + live shelves | empty-shelf notice |

Everything else — the Payment Issue Facility line, NOFRA/Visakhapatnam, the
cart with its real count and total, the search field, the drawer behaviour,
the full-width breadcrumb, the five-item bottom bar — is the same code on
both.

## Fixed wording

`FACILITY_LINE = 'Payment Issue Facility'` and
`LOCATION = { name: 'NOFRA', city: 'Visakhapatnam' }`, both exported from
`ShopScreen.tsx` and used nowhere else as literals.

An approved mockup prints **"Payment in Facility"**. That wording was
corrected in writing afterwards, so the correction is what ships, on both
shops and in the admin portal. A test fails if the old form reappears.

## What the drawer holds, and where the rail is

Corrected 18 October against the supplied Oil & Ghee mockup.

Grocers has two depths, and the drawer narrows as you go in:

| Where | Drawer | Rail beside the content | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/pif/grocers` | the 19 mains | none | the 19 tiles + live shelves |
| `/pif/grocers/:main` | that main's subcategories | that main's subcategories | its subcategory tiles |
| `/pif/grocers/:main/:sub` | that main's subcategories | that main's subcategories | banner + product area |

The drawer held the 19 mains everywhere until 18 October. That made moving
between siblings a three-step trip: out to Grocers, back into Oil & Ghee,
then pick. Now the siblings are always one tap away, either in the drawer or
in the rail that stays beside the content at phone width.

Fresh Mart is one depth, so its whole catalogue — ten entries under two
headings — sits in the drawer at once and it has no rail. Same component,
same selection, same highlight, same image rules.

The drawer opens BELOW the header, so the logo, the facility line, the
delivery point, the search field and the cart stay usable while it is open.
Its heading is the open main category's name.

## What is deliberately absent

- **No product, price, stock, count or sort control.** No Grocers product is
  assigned to these 19 categories yet, so the shelf says it is empty. The
  only number either screen prints is the cart's own, from the real cart.
- **No location picker.** The mockup draws a chevron beside NOFRA, which
  promises a choice. There is one delivery point and the app stores no
  address to switch to, so it is text. It becomes a control the day a second
  delivery point exists.

## Adding artwork to the next category

1. Crop the supplied sheet to one 800×800 WebP per category, named for its
   slug, under `public/brands/grocers/categories/<main-slug>/`.
2. Add the slugs to `GROCERS_IMAGES` in
   `src/lib/businesses/category-images.ts`.
3. Archive the untouched original under `docs/assets/grocers-source/`.
4. The screens need no change: `categoryImage()` is already consulted by the
   drawer, the grid and the hero, and falls back to the lucide icon when it
   returns null.

Do not change `category-tree.ts` by hand — it is generated by
`scripts/gen-categories.cjs`, which validates every icon name against the
icon record the sidebar actually renders from and rejects duplicate slugs.
The approved structure is 19 mains and 135 subcategories and artwork must
not reshape it.

## Verified, 17 October 2026

273 tests across 13 files; TypeScript and ESLint clean; production build
469 kB. A real Chromium run at 390 px and 1280 px exercised the logo, the
facility line, NOFRA, the 19-tile home grid, the drawer (open, Escape,
select, close), the subcategory grid, the breadcrumb, the cart, the search,
browser back, the five-item bar, an unknown category, Fresh Mart and a RIK
screen: 50 checks, all passed, no broken image responses.

`apps/api/src`, `database/`, `apps/customer-web/src/routes/rik` and
`apps/customer-web/src/lib/cart` are byte-identical to the known-good copy.
`apps/admin-web/src` differs in exactly one line — the terminology
correction above. No migration was written or run.
